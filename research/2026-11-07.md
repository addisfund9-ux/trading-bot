# Daily research — 2026-11-07

**Headline:** No API changes break the bot today; the ideas set your daily trade cap from your own results, add a short gap after every trade (wins too), and flag coins whose futures price runs far above spot.

Focus today: trading discipline (overtrading) and scanner signal quality, plus the usual API check.

## Ideas

### 1. Daily trade cap from your own history
- **Area:** discipline · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** /week groups your past days by how many trades you took (1–2, 3–4, 5–6, 7+) and shows win rate and P&L for each. The cap in tilt.py becomes the count where your days turn red, instead of a fixed 8. Your own numbers, not a rule from a book.
- **Notes:** JournalPlus's overtrading guide makes this its first step: group journal days by trade count and compare. Its example table (illustrative, not a study) shows 1–2 trade days positive and 7+ trade days negative, with the turn at 4. It then sets a "soft cap" one or two below the hard cap that forces a 30-minute break. Build: `store.py` already has the trades; bucket by Addis date, print a small table in /week, and suggest `max_trades` = the last bucket with positive average P&L (only once there are enough days in each bucket, e.g. 5+). The 08:00 brief can print the cap.

### 2. 5-minute gap after every trade, not just losses
- **Area:** discipline · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** tilt.py only flags entries within 15 min of a loss. Overtrading also starts after wins: the click itself feels good. Flag any entry within 5 min of the last exit, count them in /tilt, and show the gap rule in the morning brief.
- **Notes:** All three discipline pages read today recommend a fixed pause after every trade, win or loss (The Mental Edge: at least 5 minutes with a timer; Mind the Market: a 5–10 minute lockout; JournalPlus: 5-minute post-trade cooldown, 15 after a loss). None cites a study for the exact number, so treat 5 minutes as a starting setting. The Mental Edge argues the reward comes from entering, not from winning, which fits the 25 Oct pattern where the run started while still green. Build: in `tilt.analyse`, add a `min_gap=5` check against the previous trade's close regardless of P&L, label it "Quick re-entry", keep "Revenge" for the after-loss case.

### 3. "Premium" flag: futures price far above or below spot
- **Area:** scanner · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** The scanner already pulls /fapi/v1/premiumIndex but only uses funding. Its markPrice vs indexPrice gives the live perp-vs-spot gap. A big gap on a mover means the move is mostly leveraged futures traders, which tends to unwind. Show it next to Crowded.
- **Notes:** premium = (markPrice − indexPrice) / indexPrice. Both sources tie the premium directly to funding (funding is computed from it), but the premium is live while `lastFundingRate` is the last settled value. The Wickra docs describe a persistent premium as crowded longs and say extreme premiums often unwind as funding drains them; neither source gives a numeric threshold. Suggest judging it against the coin's own recent range (or the median across all movers today) rather than a fixed number. No extra API calls: the fields are already in the response used by `scanner.py` (line ~173) and `webapp/index.html`. Note the TradingView script uses the opposite sign convention, so keep "positive = perp above spot".
