## Ideas

### 1. /skip: log the trades you didn't take
- **Area:** discipline · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** One tap logs a trade you nearly took but skipped, with the reason (no stop, chasing, after a loss). The 21:00 reminder and /week count skips next to trades, and the evening scorecard shows how the skipped coin moved. Not trading becomes something you can see and score.
- **Notes:** The HackerNoon author suggests keeping a two-week log of trades nearly taken and then skipped because a checklist item failed; he says his mood item (no trading below 6 of 10) catches the most bad trades. The bot already counts trades, revenge trades and overtrading, but a day with restraint looks the same as a quiet day. Build: `/skip SYMBOL long|short` plus an inline keyboard of reasons (no stop / chasing / after a loss / over cap / tired); store.py gets a `skips` table (time, symbol, side, reason, price from /fapi/v1/ticker/24hr or the latest 1m kline). The 21:00 reminder adds "Skipped: 3 · Taken: 6". The evening scorecard prices each skip at the time of the skip and at +60 min, and says plainly that a skip that would have won is not a mistake if the reason was valid. Keep it private: no PnL claims, just counts and moves.

### 2. "Same bet" warning on the morning list
- **Area:** scanner · **Effort:** medium
- **Why:** Many alts move with BTC, so three longs on three alts can act like one big long. The scanner measures each pick's 1h correlation with BTC and, when most picks point the same way and move with BTC, says so at the top: treat them as one trade and size the total, not each.
- **Notes:** The Turtle Signal correlation page makes the point that "symbol diversification is not risk diversification": in crypto, BTC, ETH and many large altcoins move together in stress, and taking every correlated signal at full size overloads the account. It suggests grouping related instruments, capping risk per group, and recording signals skipped because the group was full. It gives no numbers, so thresholds here are a starting guess to tune. Build: scanner.py already pulls 1h klines for the EMA 20/50 check; compute the Pearson correlation of each coin's 1h returns against BTCUSDT over the last 48 bars (no new endpoints). Mark a pick "moves with BTC" above about 0.7. If 3 or more picks are on the same side of their EMA alignment and moves-with-BTC, show one line in the brief and the Mini App: "4 longs, all tied to BTC — this is one trade." This differs from the 1 Nov total-exposure idea, which watches open positions; this warns before any trade is opened.
