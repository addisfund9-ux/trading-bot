## Ideas

### 1. Break-even win rate next to your real one
- **Area:** risk · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** A high win rate can still lose if losses are bigger than wins. /report and /week would show "you need 52% wins to break even after fees, you're at 44%", worked out from your own average win, loss and fees, so you see when small wins are hiding a leak.
- **Notes:** LuxAlgo: "A 70% win rate can still lose money if the losses are sufficiently large." InvesClubX makes the same point (most traders lose despite winning more than half their trades) and uses avg win / avg loss as the payoff ratio b. Formula: break-even win rate p* = (L + F) / (W + L), where W = average winning trade, L = average losing trade (absolute), F = average round-trip fee per trade. Build: `review.stats()` already returns `win_rate`; add `avg_win`, `avg_loss`, `avg_fee` and `breakeven` there, then print one line in `day_review()`/`week_text()`: "Win rate 44% · need 52% to break even (avg win 0.61, avg loss 0.83, fees 0.06)". Only show it once there are 20+ closed trades in the window, otherwise say "not enough trades yet". This differs from the 3 Nov "big-loser check" (median vs total) and the October fee-drag line: it puts the two numbers you can act on side by side.

### 2. Max leverage that fits the coin's noise
- **Area:** risk · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** Each scanner card would show the highest leverage where a normal 1.5× ATR swing on 15m costs under, say, 30% of margin. On a jumpy coin that might be 12×, so reaching for 40× after a loss shows up as clearly too big before you enter.
- **Notes:** Algorithmic Token argues a stop is a measurement of normal noise ("the trade is wrong if price moves one and a half times the normal range against me") and that size, not stop width, should absorb volatility. LuxAlgo: there is "no universal mapping from ATR percentages to safe leverage" — so the bot should compute it per coin. Build: `picks.py` already computes `risk = 1.5 × atr15` and `verdict()` already blocks when liquidation comes before the stop. Add `stop_pct = risk / entry * 100` and `max_lev = floor(MAX_MARGIN_HIT / stop_pct)` with `MAX_MARGIN_HIT = 30` (percent of margin lost if the stop is hit), capped at the liquidation limit. Show it on the card as "Fits ≤ 12×" and in the Mini App risk check as WARN when the chosen leverage is above it. tilt.py could also count trades this week opened above the card's "fits" number.
