## Ideas

### 1. Tell me when the tilt guard goes quiet
- **Area:** bot · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** If the guard's Binance calls start failing (PC clock drift, key or API change), it only writes a log line, so you trade thinking it's watching. Alert in Telegram after ~2 minutes of errors and again when it's back, and sync the clock with Binance's server time.
- **Notes:** In `bot.py`, `guard_job` catches every exception from `LIVE.poll`, counts it in `GUARD_STATE["errors"]` and calls `log.warning` only on the 1st and 30th error. Nothing goes to Telegram. The only message you ever get is the one-time "Tilt guard is OFF" for a bad key. The guard is the main live protection against revenge trades and flips (`guard.py` has the flip-after-loss and cooldown alerts), so a silent outage in the middle of a session is the worst moment to lose it. Build:
  - When `errors` reaches 6 (6 × 20 s = 2 min), send "🛡 Guard can't reach Binance since HH:MM, so no tilt alerts until it's back: <short error>". When a poll succeeds after that, send "🛡 Guard back". Keep a `down_since` timestamp in `GUARD_STATE` so it sends only once per outage.
  - `guard.Client.signed` uses the local `time.time()` with `recvWindow=10000`. A Windows clock that drifts more than about 10 s gets error -1021 on every signed call. Fetch `/fapi/v1/time` once at startup and every hour, store the offset, and add it to `timestamp`. Show the offset in `/guard`.
  - `/guard` can also show "last good poll: 14 s ago".

### 2. Data health line on every scan
- **Area:** scanner · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** When an OI, taker or long/short call fails, the scanner quietly drops that part of the score, or the whole coin. Show "OI missing for 12 of 30 coins" on /scan, the Mini App and the 08:00 brief, so a Binance change or rate limit shows up on day one.
- **Notes:** In `scanner.py`, `safe()` returns `None` on any exception, and `derivs()` then leaves `oi_ch4`, `taker` or `lsr` as `None`. `scan().one()` also returns `None` on any exception, so a coin whose klines fail just disappears from the list. A Binance change to one of the `/futures/data` endpoints, or a 429/418 rate-limit ban, would make the morning picks quietly worse without any visible error. Build:
  - Have `derivs()` record which of the three fields came back `None`. Have `scan()` count the coins dropped by an exception and return `"health": {"coins_tried": 30, "coins_ok": 28, "oi_missing": 0, "taker_missing": 12, "lsr_missing": 0, "http_errors": {"429": 3}}`.
  - Add one line to `scan_text` and the brief, shown only when something is missing, e.g. "⚠️ Data: taker ratio missing for 12/30 coins; scores lean on EMAs only". Show the same thing in `webapp/index.html` (it does its own fetches, so count failures there too).
  - Optional: store the daily health in store.py. Three bad mornings in a row would point to an API change rather than a hiccup, and the brief can say so.
  - This replaces part of what the daily changelog check does, and it works even on days when that check can't run (like today).
