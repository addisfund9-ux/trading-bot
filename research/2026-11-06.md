# Daily research — 2026-11-06

**Headline:** No API changes break the bot today and research was thin, so there are just two ideas: check whether you cut winners faster than losers, and judge funding against each coin's own normal instead of one fixed number.

## Ideas

### 1. Winners vs losers: hold time and size
- **Area:** discipline · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** Traders tend to grab small wins fast and sit in losers. /report and /week would show median minutes and average USDT for winning vs losing trades, e.g. "wins 3 min, +0.40 · losses 11 min, -0.95", so you see if your wins are too small to pay for your losses.
- **Notes:** Schatzmann & Haslhofer found the disposition effect (selling winners early, holding losers) among Bitcoin investors, with varying intensity and most visible from 2017 on. The bot already flags losers held over the max-hold rule (review.py `rule_checks`, lessons.py `held_loser`), which covers half of the effect. The missing half is winners closed too early. Build: in review.py, split positions by pnl sign, then add one line with median `minutes(p)` and mean pnl for each side, plus the ratio avg win / avg loss. In /week, show the trend across days. No new data is needed; it uses only imported positions.

### 2. Crowded flag from each coin's own funding history
- **Area:** scanner · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** Crowded now fires at a fixed 0.05% funding. Some coins sit near that every day; others rarely pass 0.01%. Compare current funding to the coin's last 30 days and flag only when it's far outside its normal. Evidence that funding predicts moves is weak, so treat it as a 'size down' warning.
- **Notes:** scanner.py line ~150 uses `f_pct >= 0.05` (or `<= -0.05` for shorts). Build: for the shortlisted coins only, fetch `GET /fapi/v1/fundingRate?symbol=X&limit=90` (about 30 days at 8h intervals; some coins settle every 4h or 1h, so 90 rows covers less time). Compute a z-score of `lastFundingRate` against those rows and set crowd_f when |z| ≥ 3 in the trade direction, keeping the 0.05% rule as a fallback. Cache results for an hour so the scanner doesn't hammer the endpoint. Brave New Coin's test on BitMEX data found the funding→return link "loose" (R² ≈ 0.001), with reversals only in the most extreme cases (beyond ±3 SD). Yellow.com notes funding "can remain elevated during strong trends." So this should stay a caution flag for size and leverage, not a signal to fade.
