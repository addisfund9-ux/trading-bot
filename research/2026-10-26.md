## Ideas

### 1. Leverage ceiling that steps down after each loss
- **Area:** discipline · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** Studies of futures traders find individuals take more risk after morning losses to get back to even. Start the day at a 20× cap and halve it after each loss (20→10→5); /tilt and the report flag any trade above the cap. On 25 Oct you went the other way, up to 40×.
- **Notes:** Huang & Chan (2014) found active individual traders on Taiwan futures show a "break-even effect" after both large and small morning losses, and a "house money" effect after big gains. That is the 25 Oct pattern. tilt.py already detects leverage escalation; this turns it into a rule stated in advance. The 08:00 brief could print "Today's cap: 20×", and the cap could be configurable. Since the bot has no live API key, this is report-side (flag after import) unless the live tilt guard is built.

### 2. Liquidation vs noise check on each coin
- **Area:** risk · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** Show each coin's 15m ATR as a % of price next to how far liquidation sits at 10×, 20× and 40×. At 40× liquidation is under 2.5% away, so on a coin whose normal 15m candle moves 1%, two ordinary candles can end the trade.
- **Notes:** The approximate isolated liquidation distance is 1/leverage minus the maintenance margin rate (Binance: maintenance margin = notional × MMR − maintenance amount, with MMR rising with position size). LuxAlgo notes that published ATR-stop practice runs about 1.5× ATR for short-term trades up to 3× for trend-following, and that many early stop-outs are noise hits. A simple line on each scanner card would do: "15m ATR 0.9% · liq ≈ 9.5% / 4.5% / 2.0% at 10/20/40×", turning red when liq is within 2× ATR. scanner.py already computes a 1h ATR, and adding a 15m ATR uses the 15m klines it already fetches.

### 3. Require OI above its 20-hour average for Clean setups
- **Area:** scanner · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** The scanner already downloads 25 hours of open interest per coin. Requiring OI to sit above its 20h average, not just be up over 4h, would drop movers in thin, low-participation chop, a filter some 15m EMA + OI scripts use against fake-outs. No extra API calls.
- **Notes:** A TradingView script pairing a 15m EMA 9/15 cross with open interest only signals when OI is above its 20-period average, falling back to volume when OI is missing. In scanner.py, `derivs()` already has the 25 `sumOpenInterestValue` points, so this is a few lines plus a new flag (e.g. "OI thin") added to `clean_picks()`'s bad set. Keep the same logic in webapp/index.html.
