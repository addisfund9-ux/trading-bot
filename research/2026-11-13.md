# Daily research — 2026-11-13

**Headline:** No API change affects the bot today and the research was thin, so there are two ideas: rule changes you make mid-day wait until tomorrow, and a one-tap "did I follow my plan?" grade at 21:00 that /week sets next to your P&L.

Focus today: trading psychology and discipline tools, plus the API checks.

## Ideas

### 1. Rule changes wait until tomorrow
- **Area:** discipline · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** Your limits (leverage, trade cap, loss limit) live in .env, so a restart after a loss can loosen them on the spot. Have the bot save each day's limits at 08:00; any looser value seen later that day is ignored until the next brief, which says what you changed.
- **Notes:** bot.py reads `MAX_LEVERAGE`, `MAX_TRADES_PER_DAY`/`PLAN_MAX_TRADES`, `DAILY_LOSS_LIMIT`, `MAX_POSITION_USD`, `RISK_PER_TRADE_USD`, `COOLDOWN_MIN` and `LAST_ENTRY_TIME` from the environment at startup. Nothing stops an edit plus restart in the middle of a bad session. Build: a `limits` table in store.py keyed by date. The 08:00 job writes the day's values. On startup, compare each value with today's row. If the new value is *stricter* (lower leverage, fewer trades), apply it at once. If it's *looser*, keep today's stored value and queue the new one. The next brief prints "Changed yesterday 14:32: MAX_LEVERAGE 10 → 20". /tilt can count loosening changes per week. TradeLux suggests "20 trades before changing a core rule", which would be a stricter follow-up: allow a loosening only after 20 trades under the current rules. The Observer Mindset piece makes the same point from the other side: write the day's rules (trade count, max loss) before the open, not during it.

### 2. Nightly plan grade, 1 to 5
- **Area:** discipline · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** Add five buttons to the 21:00 reminder: "How well did you follow your plan today?" Store the tap; /week shows each day's grade next to its P&L and trade count. You learn whether your red days are bad-luck days or broken-plan days, in your own words.
- **Notes:** TradeLux recommends scoring each session "1 to 5" on plan adherence, emotional control and review quality. One question is enough here, so it gets answered. The 21:00 reminder already exists (`EVENING_REMINDER`). Add an InlineKeyboardMarkup with callback data `grade:1`…`grade:5`, plus a CallbackQueryHandler that writes (date, grade) to store.py. This is different from the 29 Oct morning mood check: that one asks how you feel before trading, while this asks how you behaved after. Over a few weeks /week can show average P&L on 4–5 days vs 1–2 days, and whether low grades line up with tilt.py's revenge or flip flags. If the self-grade says 5 on a day tilt.py flagged 6 revenge trades, show both side by side, without comment.
