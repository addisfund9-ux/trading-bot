## Ideas

### 1. Trade budget countdown in the tilt guard
- **Area:** discipline · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** Today the guard only speaks up after trade 9, when the damage is already done. Set a budget in the 08:00 brief (say 5) and get "3 of 5 left" after every new position, with each loss using up one extra. On 25 Oct the budget would have run out early in the revenge run.
- **Notes:** guard.py already tracks `opens_today` and sends `count` only when `opens_today > max_trades`. The countdown would send a short message on each new open and lower the remaining budget by one more after a losing close. The budget could be picked with buttons in the morning brief (3 / 5 / 8) and stored per day in store.py. tilt.py's overtrading flag (over 8/day) stays as the after-the-fact check.

### 2. Market breadth line at the top of the scanner
- **Area:** scanner · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** Count how many of the scanned movers have 1h EMA 20 above EMA 50 and show "Breadth 70% up" in the scanner and the brief. Flag a short when most of the market is aligned up, or a long when most is aligned down. It reuses klines the scanner already fetches.
- **Notes:** The scanner already checks direction against BTC. Breadth adds a second market check that BTC alone can miss, for example altcoins broadly rolling over while BTC is flat. Neither scanner.py nor webapp/index.html has a breadth measure yet. The coin list is the movers, not the whole market, so label it "breadth of today's movers". A neutral band (say 40–60%) should show no flag.
