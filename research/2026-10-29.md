## Ideas

### 1. One-tap mood check in the 08:00 brief
- **Area:** discipline · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** A study of 80 day traders found those with the strongest emotional swings after wins and losses traded significantly worse. Add 4 buttons to the brief (calm / OK / tense / tired) and later show your PnL and rule breaks by mood, so you learn which mornings to trade small or skip.
- **Notes:** Lo, Repin & Steenbarger surveyed 80 day traders' emotional state daily for five weeks and matched it to their normalised daily PnL. Traders whose emotional reactions to gains and losses were more intense, in both directions, had significantly worse performance. No "trader personality" profile showed up, which suggests the skill can be trained. Build: add an InlineKeyboardMarkup with 4 buttons to `morning_brief()`; a CallbackQueryHandler saves (date, mood) in a new store.py table. Optionally send the same buttons again after a loss the tilt guard sees, to capture mood mid-session. In `/week`, group days by morning mood: days, net PnL, revenge trades and trades over the daily limit. It needs about 2–3 weeks of taps before the split means anything, so show "n days" next to each mood. If you skip the tap, store nothing rather than guessing.

### 2. /size: position size from your stop, not your leverage
- **Area:** risk · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** Type /size 1.25 1.23 (entry, stop) and the bot works out the position that loses a fixed amount, e.g. 1 USDT, at the stop, then the margin at 10/20/40×. Leverage then only changes margin, not what you can lose, and the bot warns if liquidation sits before your stop.
- **Notes:** Kraken's guide: position size = (account × risk %) / |entry − stop|. It suggests 0.5–2% risk per trade and says "Leverage changes how much margin you need to hold a position, not how much you should risk on it." Worked example for the command: risk 1 USDT, entry 1.25, stop 1.23 → stop distance 1.6% → notional ≈ 62.5 USDT → margin ≈ 6.25 / 3.13 / 1.56 USDT at 10/20/40×. Isolated liquidation is roughly 1/leverage minus the maintenance margin rate away (about 2.1% at 40× for a small position), so the bot should print a red warning when that is closer than the stop. Make the risk amount a .env setting (e.g. `RISK_PER_TRADE=1`), and after a loss the tilt guard could reply with the same /size line to slow the next entry down. This is a calculator only; it places nothing.

### 3. "New" label for recently listed coins
- **Area:** scanner · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** Coins listed less than about 33 days ago don't have enough 4h candles for EMA 200, so the scanner quietly scores them lower with no reason shown. Label them "New" so you know why, and treat them as extra risky: half of Binance perp listings peaked on day one.
- **Notes:** In scanner.py, `klines()` asks for 300 bars and `ema()` returns nothing when there are fewer than 200 closes, so `trend()` returns 0 for the 4h frame and the coin can never be `full` (never Clean). Between 200 and 300 bars the EMA 200 exists but is seeded on short history. Build: in `analyse()`, return `bars4h = len(data["4h"]["close"])`; in `score()`, add a "New" flag when it is under 300 (about 50 days). No extra API calls. Mirror it in webapp/index.html. BitMEX's Q1 2025 report found 50% of new Binance perp listings hit their all-time high on day one, with a median gain to peak of only 1.02%, so a fresh listing on the movers list is more often a fading pump than a trend. The report is from an exchange promoting its own listings, so treat the numbers as indicative. Several of the contracts in today's leverage cut (below) are recent listings, which fits.
