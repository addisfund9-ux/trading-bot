## Ideas

### 1. Speed-up alert: entries coming faster
- **Area:** discipline · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** Revenge runs start as speed before they show up as losses. If three entries land inside 20 minutes, or your last gaps are under a third of your normal gap, the tilt guard pings you, win or lose. On 25 Oct you made 9 trades in an hour, which is this exact pattern.
- **Notes:** This is different from the 7 Nov "5-minute gap after every trade" idea (a fixed pause) and from the revenge-window check (an entry within 15 minutes of a *loss*). It watches the pace itself. TradesViz suggests tracking "time gaps between trades" as an early tilt sign; The Mental Edge describes overtrading as an urge to click that friction slows down. Build: guard.py already counts `opens_today` and entry times; keep a list of today's entry timestamps and alert (once per run) when 3 entries fall within 20 minutes, or when the median of the last 3 gaps is under a third of his usual gap (median gap from the last 30 days in store.py). Message: "⚡ 3 entries in 14 min. Your normal gap is 38 min." No API change.

### 2. Heat check button on every tilt alert
- **Area:** discipline · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** Each tilt alert asks one question with two buttons: "Calm" or "Heated" (fast breathing, tight jaw, thinking "I need to win it back"). Heated starts a 15-minute walk-away timer. /week shows how trades taken after a Heated tap turned out.
- **Notes:** TradesViz lists early signs: "rapid breathing, clenched jaw, tunnel vision" and thoughts that start with "I need to" or "I have to". Briscoe describes a mid-trade "temperature check"; Steenbarger says tilt is preceded by frustration and negative self-talk, so catching it early is the point. TradesViz also notes "even 10 minutes of walking" helps. Build: in bot.py add an inline keyboard (Calm / Heated) to the guard's alert messages; a CallbackQueryHandler stores (time, answer) in a new `heat` table in store.py; Heated replies with a 15-minute timer and the morning plan line. /week joins taps to the next trade's result: "After Heated: 4 trades, 1 win." Keep it two taps, no typing. The 08:00 mood check covers the morning; this covers the moment.

### 3. Review before trading the day after a tilt session
- **Area:** discipline · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** If yesterday had a tilt session, the 08:00 brief asks three short questions first: what set it off, the first sign, and what you'll do at that sign next time. The picks show after you answer, and today's trade cap is halved.
- **Notes:** TradesViz's advice after a tilt session: stop, take at least a full day off, review, then trade smaller. Briscoe: "Spot the pattern. Name what's going on. Zero judgment." The bot already finds tilt sessions in tilt.py; today nothing changes the next morning. Build: in brief.py, if yesterday's rows contain a tilt session, send three questions first (ForceReply or a short conversation), save the answers in store.py, and only then send the picks. Set today's `max_trades` in the guard to half (rounded down, minimum 1). On the next tilt alert, show his own answer to question 3 ("What I'll do at the first sign"). Differs from the 3 Nov if-then plan (written once, in advance) because this one is written about the actual session, the morning after.
