# Daily research — 2026-11-15

**Headline:** No API change affects the bot today; the ideas make you write a one-line plan before each trade, make a losing coin wait for a fresh setup instead of a timer, and have you pick one trading hour in the morning brief.

Focus today: trading psychology and discipline tools (pre-trade checklists, planning prompts), plus the API checks.

## Ideas

### 1. /plan before every entry
- **Area:** discipline · **Effort:** medium
- **Why:** Before you open a trade, send '/plan SOL long stop 142.1'. The guard checks each new position against it: no plan in the last 10 minutes, or a stop that doesn't match, and you get an 'unplanned' alert. /week shows planned vs unplanned P&L.
- **Notes:** All four checklist pages say the same thing: write setup, stop and size down *before* the entry. The Observer Mindset adds that the urge often passes while you write. The 13 Nov notes dropped a pre-trade checklist because the bot can't block entries. This version doesn't block anything. It uses what guard.py already reads with the read-only key: `/fapi/v2/positionRisk` for new positions and `openOrders` / `openAlgoOrders` for stops. Build: a `/plan <coin> <long|short> stop <price> [target <price>]` command stores a row (time, symbol, side, stop) in store.py. In `Guard.update`, when a new position appears, look for a matching plan from the last 10 minutes. If there is none, send "unplanned". If the stop order's price is more than ~0.2% from the planned stop, send "stop moved from plan". Each plan is used once, so after a loss you have to write a new one. Trader's Second Brain's readiness questions ("re-entering without a new setup?", "increasing size to recover a loss?") can be printed in the reply to /plan when the last trade was a loss. /week: count, P&L and win rate for planned vs unplanned trades.

### 2. After a loss, the coin waits for a new setup
- **Area:** scanner · **Effort:** medium
- **Why:** Revenge trades are usually the same coin, same idea, minutes later. After a loss, mark that coin 'used' on the scanner and in the guard until its 15m EMA 9/21 crosses back and re-aligns: a real new setup, not just 15 minutes passing.
- **Notes:** This idea comes from Trader's Second Brain's gate "re-entering without a new setup?". The current rules are time-based (`COOLDOWN_MIN`) and count-based (`MAX_LOSSES_PER_COIN`). Neither knows whether the chart has actually changed. On 25 Oct the flips were on the same coins inside the hour. Build: when guard.py sees a losing close, store (symbol, side, close time). The scanner already pulls 15m klines and computes EMA 9/21. A coin stays "used" for that side until EMA 9 has crossed to the other side of EMA 21 and then crossed back in the trade's direction, after the loss. The scanner card and the Mini App show a grey "Used — no new setup yet" badge. If a position opens on a used coin, the guard alert says "same setup as the trade you just lost". It's a label, not a block. Clear all marks at 08:00.

### 3. Pick your one trading hour at 08:00
- **Area:** discipline · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** The brief asks you to tap one window for today (09–10, 10–11 or 11–12) inside your 12:00 cutoff. The guard warns on any entry outside it. Naming a narrow window ahead of time is a planning prompt that has raised follow-through in a field study.
- **Notes:** Mazar, Mochon & Ariely (2018, Journal of Consumer Psychology) found that a "minimalist planning prompt" helped people pay overdue bills in a field experiment. The prompt asked people to name a narrower time window ("if you are going to pay within the next 24 hours, press 1"). The abstract read today doesn't give the effect size. Thürmer et al. (2020) found that if-then plans weakened the link between automatic urges and impulse buying. That was a shopping task, not trading, so treat it as supporting evidence only. The bot already has `LAST_ENTRY_TIME` (12:00) and `MAX_TRADES` (3). This narrows the window by your own choice each morning. Build: an InlineKeyboardMarkup on the 08:00 brief with three or four one-hour buttons plus "Rest day". Store the tap by date. Guard: an entry outside the chosen hour sends "outside the hour you picked (10–11)". At the end of the hour, send "your hour is over". /week: on days you picked an hour, how many trades were inside vs outside it. With no tap, nothing changes and the 12:00 rule still applies.
