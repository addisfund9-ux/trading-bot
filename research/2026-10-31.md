# Daily research — 2026-10-31

**Headline:** No API changes break the bot today; the ideas add weekend rules to the brief, show when each coin's funding is charged and how often, and split your results into coins from the morning list versus coins you chased.

## Ideas

### 1. Weekend rules in the Saturday and Sunday brief
- **Area:** risk · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** Weekend crypto volume is now only about 13% of the week, so books on small coins are thinner and wicks run further. On Sat/Sun the brief could switch to weekend rules: fewer trades, a lower leverage cap, and low-volume movers hidden from Clean picks.
- **Notes:** Kaiko data (via Decrypt, Feb 2024) put the weekend share of bitcoin volume at 13% in early 2024, down from 17% in 2023 and 24% in 2018, with weekend spreads widening. Bitsgap (Oct 2026, citing Kaiko via CryptoSlate) says weekday volume ran at about twice weekend levels in 2025–26, that spreads widen mostly on smaller pairs while BTC/ETH barely change, and that market orders slip more. Bitsgap sells trading bots, so treat its figures as indicative. Build: in `morning_brief()`, check the weekday in Africa/Addis_Ababa. On Saturday and Sunday, print a "Weekend rules" block with a lower trade cap (e.g. 2 instead of 3, as a `WEEKEND_MAX_TRADES` .env setting) and a lower leverage cap (e.g. `WEEKEND_MAX_LEV=20`), and have /tilt mark weekend trades that broke them. In scanner.py and webapp/index.html, raise the minimum 24h quote volume for Clean picks on weekends (or apply the 27 Oct "Thin" spread flag if it gets built). After a few weekends, /week can compare your weekend and weekday PnL so the thresholds come from your own data. Note Friday-to-Monday funding is still charged as normal.

### 2. Funding clock on each scanner card
- **Area:** scanner · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** Some coins charge funding every 4h or 1h, not 8h, and at 40× one 0.1% payment costs 4% of your margin. Show "funding 0.10%/4h, in 12 min" on each card and flag high funding about to settle, using data the scanner already fetches plus one free call.
- **Notes:** `GET /fapi/v1/premiumIndex` (already called once for all symbols, weight 10) returns `nextFundingTime` next to `lastFundingRate`, but scanner.py only keeps the rate. `GET /fapi/v1/fundingInfo` has 0 IP weight and returns only the symbols whose cap, floor or `fundingIntervalHours` was adjusted; any symbol not listed is on the default 8h. A DEV Community post (its author sells a funding tool, numbers unverified) counted hundreds of USDT perps on 4h and a few on 1h, so newer movers often aren't on 8h. Maths: funding is charged on the position's notional, so the cost as a share of margin is rate × leverage (0.1% at 40× = 4%; at 20× = 2%). Build: keep `nextFundingTime` in `fund`, fetch fundingInfo once per scan (cache it for an hour), and show rate, interval and minutes to settlement on each card. Add a "Funding soon" flag when settlement is within 15 minutes and |rate| ≥ 0.05%, and a trade in the setup's direction would be the side paying. Also: the "Crowded" (±0.05%) and "Squeeze" (±0.1%) thresholds are per payment, so on a 4h coin the same rate costs twice as much per day — consider comparing the rate per 8h (rate × 8 / interval) instead. Mirror in webapp/index.html.

### 3. On-list vs off-list trades
- **Area:** discipline · **Effort:** small
- **Why:** Research finds people mostly buy what grabs their attention: big movers and volume spikes. Save each morning's scanner picks and split your PnL and revenge trades by coins on the list vs off it, so you can see what chasing off-plan coins costs you.
- **Notes:** Barber & Odean (Review of Financial Studies, 2008) found individual investors are net buyers of stocks in the news, stocks with unusually high volume and stocks with extreme one-day returns, because attention decides which choices they even consider. Your routine starts with a planned list, but revenge trades are often taken on whatever is moving at that moment. The bot doesn't save the morning picks today. Build: in `morning_brief()`, store (date, symbol, direction, score) for the picks in a new store.py table. After import, tag each trade "on list" if its symbol was picked that day (and "same direction" if the side matches). /report and /tilt then show: "On list: N trades, X USDT · Off list: M trades, Y USDT, of which K revenge". /week can add the 7-day totals. If off-list trades are consistently negative, the 08:00 brief can state a rule like "only coins from this list today". This needs no new input from you.
