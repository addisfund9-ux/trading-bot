    How the score works
    Score out of 100. EMA alignment 35, open interest rising with the move 12, volume rank 12, volume spike 10, taker buy/sell flow 10, strength vs BTC 8, BTC alignment 8, not crowded 5. Minus 10 when price is stretched more than 3 ATR from the 1h EMA 20.

    Flags. OI falling the move comes from positions closing, not new money. Crowded funding or the long/short ratio shows one side packed in. Stretched a late entry, wait for a pullback. vs BTC BTC is trending the other way on all timeframes. Squeeze funding beyond ±0.1%: the move is one side being forced out, and it can snap back fast.
